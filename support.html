Common questions

How do I get my history onto a new phone?

Turn on backup before you switch: open Profile, tap Back up my data, sign in with your email, and tap Back up now. On the new phone, sign in with the same email and restore. Backup only happens when you tap it, so make one just before you change phones. Without a backup, history lives only on the phone it was made on.

Why does Hearty say it is estimating my sleep?

If your watch or another app has recorded your sleep in Apple Health, Hearty uses it. Otherwise it works out a night from how still your phone was, or from your usual bedtime, and labels it as an estimate so you can correct it.

How do I stop Hearty reading Apple Health?

Open the Settings app, then Privacy and Security, then Health, then Hearty, and switch off whatever you like. Hearty stops using that data straight away.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app erases everything stored on your phone. If you turned on backup, open Back up my data inside the app and delete your account, which erases the copy we hold along with your email address. Or email us and we will do it for you.

Is Hearty medical advice?

No. Hearty is for general wellbeing and motivation, and is not a medical device. Please talk to a qualified health professional about your health.
