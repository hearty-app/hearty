Hearty is not medical advice

This is the important one. Hearty suggests daily targets based on your age, weight and activity, and shows you readings from Apple Health. All of it is for general wellbeing and motivation only.

Hearty is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not use it to diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a qualified health professional before changing how you eat, sleep or exercise, and especially if you have any existing condition. If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact emergency services.

Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep shown in Hearty come from Apple Health and the devices that wrote them there. Their accuracy is not something Hearty controls or can vouch for.

Where Hearty works something out for itself — a night estimated from how still your phone was, or one guessed from your usual bedtime — it says so on screen, and those are estimates rather than measurements.

Hearty is licensed to you for personal, non-commercial use. Please don't reverse-engineer it, resell it, or use it in a way that breaks the law.

Your data is yours, and it is your responsibility

Everything Hearty stores lives on your device unless you turn on backup, which is off until you choose it and sign in.

Without backup, there is no copy of your history anywhere but your phone and no account to restore from. If you delete the app, replace your phone, or lose it, your history is gone and cannot be recovered.

With backup on, a copy is kept under your account, and it is only ever as recent as the last time you tapped "Back up now" — nothing is sent automatically or in the background. Keeping it current is up to you. Deleting your account erases that copy, and that cannot be undone. What is in it, and who can read it, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
