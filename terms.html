Hearty — Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hearty is not medical advice

This is the important one. Hearty suggests daily targets based on your age, weight and activity, and shows you readings from Apple Health. All of it is for general wellbeing and motivation only.

Hearty is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not use it to diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a qualified health professional before changing how you eat, sleep or exercise, and especially if you have any existing condition. If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact emergency services.

Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep shown in Hearty come from Apple Health and the devices that wrote them there. Their accuracy is not something Hearty controls or can vouch for.

Where Hearty works something out for itself — a night estimated from how still your phone was, or one guessed from your usual bedtime — it says so on screen, and those are estimates rather than measurements.

Hearty is licensed to you for personal, non-commercial use. Please don't reverse-engineer it, resell it, or use it in a way that breaks the law.

Hearty Pro

Most of Hearty is free: the heart, the five daily goals, your streak, levels and the home screen widget. Hearty Pro adds Activities (workouts and lifting), Calories, Apple Health, your full history and reports, two streak freezes a month, the level world to explore, the 3D scenes over History, Track and Activities, and the Pro-only items in the Heart Shop (still bought with coins, and yours to keep once bought).

Free trial. Everything is open for 7 days from when you finish setting up. It costs nothing and asks for no payment details, and nothing is charged when it ends — the Pro features simply lock. There is one free trial per iPhone: deleting and reinstalling Hearty doesn't start a new one.

Subscription. Hearty Pro is billed monthly or yearly, at the price shown in the app before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it ends.

Cancelling. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays open until the end of the period you have paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If Pro ends, nothing you logged is deleted. It stays on your phone, and subscribing again opens it.

Coins

Coins buy outfits, emotes and animals in the Heart Shop. You earn them by logging your habits, and you can also buy packs of them in the shop's coin bank, at the price shown before you confirm, charged to your Apple ID. Coins buy nothing else: not XP, heart health, levels or Hearty Pro, and items marked Pro still need Hearty Pro to buy. They have no cash value and can't be exchanged, transferred or cashed out. Coins you bought are kept with the rest of your data, so they come back with a backup you restore, once the App Store's record confirms them (so restoring them needs a connection); like your history, they are lost if you delete Hearty without one. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your data is yours, and it is your responsibility

Everything Hearty stores lives on your device unless you turn on backup, which is off until you choose it and sign in.

Without backup, there is no copy of your history anywhere but your phone and no account to restore from. If you delete the app, replace your phone, or lose it, your history is gone and cannot be recovered.

With backup on, a copy is kept under your account, and it is only ever as recent as the last time you tapped "Save my progress" — nothing is sent automatically or in the background. Keeping it current is up to you. Deleting your account erases that copy, and that cannot be undone. What is in it, and who can read it, is set out in the Privacy Policy.
