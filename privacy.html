Hearty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

Hearty keeps everything on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on backup — and backup is off until you choose it.

What Hearty stores on your phone

  • About you — your name, optionally a last name, your age, height, weight, gender, and the kind of work day you have. Optionally a profile photo, which stays in the app's own folder on the phone.
  • What you log — steps, water, meals, mindfulness minutes, sleep times, workouts and lifting sets, weigh-ins, food entries, and pulse checks you count yourself. Sleep may come from your own entry or, if you connect it, from Apple Health.
  • Your progress — XP, level, heart health, goals, reminder times, and whether you prefer dark mode.

If you turn on backup

Backup is optional and off until you sign in. It exists so that a new phone, or a reinstall, can get your history back.

When you sign in, we email a code to the address you give us to check the account is yours. That address is stored so you can sign back in later.

Tapping "Back up now" sends a copy of everything listed above to our database, which is hosted by Supabase. It is stored under your account, and the database is configured so that only you, signed in, can read it.

Your heart readings are never included. Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability are read from Apple Health each time you look at them and are never saved by Hearty, so there is nothing of them to upload.

Sleep is different. If Apple Health has a night your watch or phone recorded, Hearty saves it the same way it saves a night you logged by hand — so once it is in your history it is part of a backup like everything else.

Nothing is sent automatically, on a schedule, or in the background. A backup happens only when you ask for one.

Deleting your account, from the same screen, erases your backup and your email address from the server. That cannot be undone.

If you do not turn on backup

Then nothing leaves your phone at all. Hearty contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data-collection software of any kind, and makes no other network requests with your data.

In that case Hearty has no copy of anything. Deleting the app erases everything, permanently, with no way to recover it.

Health data

Health and fitness data is used only to show you your own progress. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Permissions Hearty asks for

  • Motion & Fitness — to count your steps.
  • Apple Health — to read heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and sleep.
  • Photos or Camera — only if you choose a profile picture.
  • Notifications — to send the reminders you set.

Every one is optional. Hearty works without them, with the matching feature switched off. You can withdraw any of them at any time in the iOS Settings app, and Hearty will stop using that data immediately.

Children

Hearty is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
